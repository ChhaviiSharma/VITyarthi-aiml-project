PROBLEM STATEMENT- It is a system which tells about the price of a house based on the space , number of bedrooms and timespan of the house .It automatically predict the price of the house with the help of simple calculation and a formula .

SCOPE OF THE PROJECT-It helps the users to know about the price of the house by calculating the space of the house , number of bedrooms and the timespan of the house . It will make the calculations easy and time effective and help the user to quickly take decisions 

TARGET USER-Target users are the people who want to purchase or rent a house and want to predict the cost of the house and also the property dealers who want to sell the house and want to know how much a house cost .
